Volatility Surface Lab: noise-free Heston market

Each expiry's smile is fitted with a raw SVI curve (a five-parameter formula for total implied variance). The fits are then checked for static arbitrage: butterfly (negative implied density inside one expiry) and calendar (total variance falling as maturity increases).
